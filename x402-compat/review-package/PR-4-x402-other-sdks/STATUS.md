# PR 4 — other SDKs (Go, Python): recommended skip

Upstream allows a chain to add mechanisms to the other-language SDKs, but does not require it,
and most registered chains have not. FINDINGS.md documents that chains with merged specs and no
mechanism package at all are normal (algo, canton, casper, starknet, sui, ton).

Recommendation: do not build these until someone asks for them. This folder exists so the
"3-4 PRs" map is complete and the decision is recorded rather than forgotten.
